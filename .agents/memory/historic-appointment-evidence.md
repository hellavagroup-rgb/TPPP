---
name: Historical appointment evidence
description: Why old allocation text must remain distinct from a verified appointment.
---

Treat legacy appointment text as historical evidence, not an authoritative reconstructed appointment. An allocation status alone is not evidence of a selected slot.

**Why:** Older real Matched records can have notes and offered options but no assignment, selection, or booking snapshot. A feature opt-in must not erase their available context or invent a selection from pending offers.

**How to apply:** Keep authorised notes visible across Matched statuses. Show verified tenant-owned assignment or selection details when available; otherwise retain recorded text with an explicit unverified label and name missing fields honestly.
