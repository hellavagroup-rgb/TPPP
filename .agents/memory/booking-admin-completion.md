---
name: CY&A booking administration
description: Combined booked-client visibility and the authority of historic administrative completion.
---

CY&A wants confirmed appointments and completed booking administration in one Booked view, distinguished by Admin outstanding / Admin complete. Completion means booking administration is finished, not that therapy has ended. Keep the other practice's existing views unchanged.

**Why:** The user found separate Confirmed and Completed views confusing and explicitly requested unification.

**How to apply:** Keep completed and outstanding clients searchable together, allow filtering by admin progress, and preserve access to insurance, registration, manual overrides and existing appointment evidence after completion or reopening.

Older confirmations, manager overrides and explicitly WriteUpp-arranged records may be administratively completed after both saved WriteUpp checks and an explicit staff review with a note. This records administrative review only. Do not manufacture payment, registration, consent or portal booking evidence, and do not relax normal portal booking completion safeguards.

**Why:** Older confirmations had no completion path because the newer route requires portal booking evidence they never collected. The user approved a separate reviewed administrative path rather than fabricating that evidence.

**How to apply:** Require practice-scoped admin authority, preserve missing-evidence warnings after completion, retain who reviewed and when, and leave appointments, payment resources and emails unchanged. Do not automatically complete live records on the user's behalf.
