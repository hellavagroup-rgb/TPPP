---
name: Automatic payment link workflow
description: Product decision for payment-link creation in the registration and booking workflow.
---

In the new workflow, payment-link creation and delivery are automatic as the client completes the process. Administrators do not need early manual payment-link access.

**Why:** A manual action duplicates the automated progression and creates uncertainty about which path staff should use.

**How to apply:** Show payment progress and failures to administrators, with recovery actions only when automation fails; do not expose routine early creation as a normal workflow step.
