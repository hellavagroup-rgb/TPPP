---
name: Future appointment promises
description: Distinguish an exact future appointment from a recurring availability window.
---

Use a staff-created one-off slot to offer an exact future date; do not bring a recurring start date forward to bypass eligibility. A recurring weekday and availability window are not verification of its first appointment occurrence.

**Why:** Families could previously see a weekday/time without enough date context, while public selection bypassed the future-start restriction applied to administrators. Calculating a weekday alone does not verify a clinician's first appointment.

**How to apply:** Keep recurring first-date wording explicitly unconfirmed unless there is separately verified appointment evidence. Preserve one-off feature opt-ins. Apply UK practice calendar dates consistently to staff and public eligibility, and recheck locked slot state before reserving it.
