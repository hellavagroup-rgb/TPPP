---
name: CY&A insurance collection timing
description: CY&A's decision about when client policy details are collected.
---

CY&A keeps collecting insurance policy numbers and authorisation codes during registration, after the client selects an appointment. Do not add those fields to intake unless CY&A changes this decision. Before registration, staff should see the recorded insurer and clearly see policy fields as not provided.

**Why:** The user confirmed the existing registration timing and declined collecting these details during intake.

**How to apply:** Keep admin visibility honest about missing data; do not imply an insurer name alone includes policy or authorisation details.

Admins must retain access to saved insurance details for Confirmed and Completed clients, not just clients in the active booking workflow.

**Why:** The user explicitly needs to retrieve policy and authorisation details after booking administration is complete.

**How to apply:** Preserve insurance visibility when changing staff views or client lifecycle stages. Keep insurer funding distinct from recorded card setup; neither an insurer name nor a saved card is proof that a session has been paid.
