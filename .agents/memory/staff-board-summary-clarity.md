---
name: Staff board summary clarity
description: Why compact client cards must retain identification context and clear responsibility for the next step.
---

Keep a brief factual client description and the next staff action or waiting party visible on Matched and Booking progress cards without opening the detail panel.

**Why:** The user rejected status-only compact cards: a practice-system reference and workflow label do not tell administrators who the client is, what they should do, or what is holding up the case. Moving all information into a panel made these columns less useful than the earlier stages.

**How to apply:** When simplifying these cards, preserve meaningful staff-entered context as well as any permitted client name. Detail panels add depth; they must not replace at-a-glance identification and next-step guidance. Derive waiting states from recorded evidence, not from historical notes or the mere existence of appointment options.

Missing payment evidence on an already confirmed booking needs a clear staff-review action, not an implication that the client must pay again.

**Why:** The user flagged a confusing waiting banner on a confirmed client's card whose historical notes reported payment completed and treatment already underway. A gap in the portal's structured evidence is not proof of nonpayment.

**How to apply:** Show an explicit payment-record review control. Keep that review read-only, preserve provider-verification opt-ins and ownership checks, and distinguish finishing booking administration from ending therapy. Do not silently mark payment active or send another payment request.
