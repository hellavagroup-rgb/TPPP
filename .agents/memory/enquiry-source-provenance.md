---
name: Original enquiry provenance
description: Why matching a contact email is not enough to identify an original enquiry.
---

Do not automatically label or link an unconverted inbox message as a client's original enquiry solely because its email matches.

**Why:** Clients created separately through staff entry can have an unconverted inbox message with the same contact email. That is a candidate for review, not evidence that this particular client was converted from it; family members can also share a contact address.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between a verified source link, conservative recovery of an already-processed orphan, and an unrelated/unconverted candidate. Missing linkage must be described honestly rather than treated as a loading failure or repaired through an unverified association. Any manual association should require explicit staff review.
