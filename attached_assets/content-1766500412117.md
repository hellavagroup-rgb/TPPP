#### Therapy enquiry form

This form enables us to plan next steps and to ensure that we are the best fit for you. We know that finding therapeutic help is anxiety provoking and we want to make sure that you are able to move forward with confidence.

This short questionnaire usually takes around 10–15 minutes to complete and helps us understand what’s been going on for you, what kind of support you’re looking for, and how to match you with the most appropriate clinician within our practice.

The information you share will be reviewed by a senior clinician and used to:

• Understand your main difficulties and current needs

• Check whether there are any urgent risk or safety concerns

• Consider any preferences or adjustments that would help you feel comfortable in therapy

• Allocate you to a clinician whose experience and availability best fits your needs

This helps us ensure you receive the right support, as quickly and safely as possible.

Everything you share in this form is treated as confidential and stored securely in line with professional and data protection standards. Your information will only be accessed by relevant members of our clinical team.

Completing this form is an alternative to an initial telephone screening call. You’re welcome to share as much or as little detail as feels comfortable for you. There are no “right” or “wrong” answers — honest responses help us support you better.

In some cases, we may get in touch to ask for more information before confirming whether we can allocate a clinician.

Please note all sessions are online.

Your personal data will be protected under GDPR legislation. Your personal details will be kept within our secure clinic systems.
