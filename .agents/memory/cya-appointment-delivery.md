---
name: CY&A appointment delivery
description: Meaning of at-practice versus online-only availability, and preservation of appointment promises.
---

For CY&A, clinicians keep two availability choices: at the practice means the time can be offered in person or online; online only means it cannot be offered in person. Keep other practices' existing interpretation unchanged.

**Why:** CY&A reported that an online-preferring client could not be matched with clinicians working from the practice. The user approved this simplified two-choice policy rather than adding a third availability choice.

**How to apply:** Separate availability capability from the actual mode selected for each new offer or booking. Do not change the client's submitted preference to bypass matching. Validate the actual online mode against any enabled Zoom requirements, even when the clinician is physically at the practice.

Existing sent offers, reservations and confirmed bookings retain their original mode. Do not retrospectively reinterpret their recorded in-person mode as online or flexible.

**Why:** The policy changes what free availability can support, not what a client has already been promised.

**How to apply:** Preserve the selected appointment mode through registration, confirmation, staff views, emails and retries. Use the historical slot mode only where no separately recorded appointment mode exists.
